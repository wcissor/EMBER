Try it in this order
1
Watch the rails fill in. All seven start hollow; the second reading lands one field at a time and the bar counts it. Nothing is tappable until a field’s reading arrives.
2
Tap any row. The crop above slides to that field’s handwriting and the caption names it.
3
Resolve Дозировка — two readings, pick either, or enter your own.
4
Send Количество to the doctor. Same one tap as typing it.
5
Try to finish early. Завершить does not exist until every field is closed — the bar tells you which are left.
6
Tap the photo for the full sheet with the active region outlined, and Ярче for glare.
What is being demonstrated
There is no code path that completes this record with an open field. The button is not disabled; it is not rendered. The slot in its place names what is outstanding.
A field cannot reach a settled state before its second reading lands, because the settled row is only reachable from a resolution action and no resolution action is rendered while the rail is hollow.
Every resolved field keeps Изменить решение, so nothing is a trap — but reopening puts the decision back in the open count and the completion control disappears again.

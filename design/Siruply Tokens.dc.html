Why seven, not six

Both faces were rendered and inspected for the full Azerbaijani Latin set and Cyrillic. Spline Sans Mono was the first choice for numerals and was rejected: its Cyrillic falls back to a serif, which is exactly the mid‑line font jump a mixed prescription would trigger.
The numeral treatment
0O 1l7 5S 8B
Every digit in the product — doses, quantities, counts, dates, record numbers — is set in Plex Mono with tabular figures. A narrow oval zero against a round O, a one with a full base serif, a five that cannot collapse into an S. Dosage errors start in typography, so the numerals get their own face rather than the text face’s defaults.
